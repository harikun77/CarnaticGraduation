The Milestone Concert

About the Graduation Concert

With the blessings of Almighty, Gurus and elders, Sahana Kumar will be performing her formal Vocal Graduation Concert on August 30th, 2026, presented by Paramacharya School of Music under the able guidance of Guru Smt. Geetha Ravi. In the Carnatic classical tradition, this milestone concert represents the culmination of 11 years of rigorous sadhana in Sruti (tonal fidelity), Laya (rhythm), Sahitya Bhava (lyrical devotion), and Manodharma Sangeetham (spontaneous improvisation).

Full Concert Program

11 Pieces • Bhairavi Varnam to Harivarasanam

Explore the curated 11-piece repertoire including the monumental Viriboni Bhairavi Ata Tala Varnam, Purandara Dasa's Gajavadana Beduve, Tyagaraja's Jnanamosagarada (Sub-Main), Dikshitar's Ananda Natana Prakasam, Swathi Thirunal's Bhogendra Sayinam, the grand centerpiece Sarojadalanetri (Main), ArabhiManam, Andal's Thiruppavai in Sindhu Bhairavi, Kavadi Chindu, Maand Thillana, and Harivarasanam.

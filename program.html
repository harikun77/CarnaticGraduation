Detailed Commentary

Song-by-Song Details & Sahityam

Filter repertoire:

1. Viriboni (Bhairavi Ata Thala Varnam)

Varnam 1

Revered across generations as the "King of all Varnams" (Varnangalil Raja), "Viriboni" is the supreme benchmark of vocal discipline, breath control, and rhythmic command in the Carnatic pantheon.

Composed in the majestic Bhashanga ragam Bhairavi and set to the intricate 14-beat Ata talam, this composition traverses all three octaves. It features structured Purvanga (Pallavi, Anupallavi, Mukthayi Swaram) and Uttaranga (Charanam and four expansive Chittaswara suites) rendered in two speeds (Prathama and Dwitiya Kalam), setting an authoritative, classical tone for the recital.

Ragam: Bhairavi (20th Melakarta Janya - Bhashanga)
Talam: Khanda Jathi Ata Talam (14 Beats)
Composer: Pacchimiriam Adiyappa

2. Gajavadana Beduve

Invocatory 2

A sparkling invocatory Devaranama in Kannada by the Father of Carnatic Music, Saint Purandara Dasa, offering heartfelt salutations to the elephant-faced Lord Ganesha to remove all obstacles and bestow auspicious blessings.

Set to the effervescent pentatonic Ragam Hamsadhwani ("the voice of the swan"), this piece radiates spiritual joy and rhythmic symmetry. The composition features vibrant Kalpanaswarams (spontaneous swara permutations) that invigorate the concert atmosphere.

Ragam: Hamsadhwani (29th Melakarta Janya)
Talam: Adi Talam
Composer: Saint Purandara Dasa

3. Jnanamosagarada (Sub-Main)

Sub-Main 3

The Sub-Main centerpiece of the first half. A profound philosophical masterpiece by Saint Tyagaraja beseeching Lord Rama: "Will You not bestow upon me supreme wisdom (Jnana) and the path of divine truth?"

Poorvikalyani is renowned for its intense emotional depth (gambhiram) and prathi madhyama curves. The presentation includes an elaborate Raga Alapana delineating hallmark phrases, followed by the kriti with brisk Neraval at "Paramathmudu" and multi-speed kalpanaswaras concluding in a synchronized makutam.

Ragam: Poorvikalyani (53rd Melakarta Janya)
Talam: Rupakam (3 Beats / 6 Matras)
Composer: Saint Tyagaraja

4. Ananda Natana Prakasam

Keerthanam 4

One of the monumental Pancha Bhoota Sthala Kritis representing the Element of Ether/Space (Akasa Lingam) at the sacred temple of Chidambaram.

Composed in majestic Sanskrit by Muthuswami Dikshitar, this grand kriti vividly describes Lord Shiva's cosmic dance of bliss (Ananda Tandavam) in the Golden Hall of Chidambaram. The stately vakra phrases of Ragam Kedaram paired with the rhythmic 7-beat Misra Chapu talam evoke cosmic majesty and deep spiritual wonder.

Ragam: Kedaram (29th Melakarta Janya)
Talam: Misra Chapu (7 Beats / 3+4)
Composer: Muthuswami Dikshitar

5. Bhogendra Sayinam

Keerthanam 5

A buoyant, melodious Sanskrit composition by the royal composer Maharaja Swathi Thirunal extolling Lord Vishnu reclining peacefully upon the celestial serpent king, Adisesha.

The unique scalar contours of Ragam Kuntalavarali (omitting Rishabham and Gandharam) combined with the lively 5-beat Khanda Chapu cycle create an undulating, wavy rhythm that mirrors the gentle sway of the ocean of milk (Ksheerasagara), complemented by cascading chittaswaras.

Ragam: Kuntalavarali (28th Melakarta Janya)
Talam: Khanda Chapu (5 Beats)
Composer: Maharaja Swathi Thirunal

6. Sarojadala Netri (Grand Main Centerpiece)

Grand Main 6

The crowning zenith and monumental centerpiece of the concert. Syama Sastri's "Sarojadala Netri" is one of the grandest gems in Carnatic music, praising the lotus-eyed Goddess Meenakshi with unmatched rhythmic architecture and lyrical majesty.

This centerpiece unfolds through structured classical Manodharma:
1. Grand Raga Alapana: An expansive, multi-octave exploration of Shankarabharanam across Mandra, Madhya, and Tara sthayis.
2. Tanam: Majestic rhythmic vocalization simulating the pluck of the Veena strings.
3. Kriti & Neraval: Thematic improvisation at "Sama Gana Vinodini" exploring Goddess Meenakshi as the lover of celestial music.
4. Kalpanaswaras: Mathematically rich swara korvais in 2-kalai Adi talam.
5. Tani Avartanam: A solo percussion presentation of complex nadais and rhythmic calculations.

Ragam: Shankarabharanam (29th Melakarta)
Talam: Adi Talam (2-Kalai - 16 Beats)
Composer: Syama Sastri (Carnatic Trinity)

7. ArabhiManam

Ragamalika 7

An expressive, soulful composition set as a vibrant Ragamalika (a garland of diverse ragas), conveying heartfelt devotion and seeking divine grace and affection (Abhimanam).

Rendered as a melodious Ragamalika, this piece seamlessly traverses contrasting melodic colors and moods, sustaining a joyful, devotional cadence following the grand centerpiece.

Ragam: Ragamalika
Talam: Adi Talam
Composer: Traditional / Classical Composition

8. Andru Ivulagam (Thiruppavai - Sindhu Bhairavi)

Thiruppavai 8

The celebrated 24th Pasuram of Andal's sacred Tamil Thiruppavai, singing praises of Lord Vishnu's cosmic footsteps measuring the entire universe (Trivikrama Avatara): "Praise be to Thy feet that measured this whole world of yore!"

Rendered in the deeply emotional and universal raga Sindhu Bhairavi, this piece captures Andal's sublime surrender and unconditional bhakti through delicate gamakas and melodious devotional poetry.

Ragam: Sindhu Bhairavi
Talam: Adi Talam
Composer: Kotai Nachiyar (Sri Andal)

9. Valli Kanavan Perai (Kavadi Chindu)

Kavadi Chindu 9

The infectious, pulsating folk-classical Kavadi Chindu dedicated to Lord Muruga: "Chant the name of Valli's consort, oh parrot, and sing His eternal glories!"

Characterized by lilting syncopated rhythms (Tisra Nadai) and joyful, catchy refrains, this piece shifts the concert mood from heavy classical manodharma into soulful, spirited regional folk traditions.

Ragam: Chenchurutti / Folk Ragamalika
Talam: Tisra Nadai / Adi
Composer: Traditional / Kavi Kunjara Bharati

10. Maand Thillana

Thillana 10

The rhythmic crescendo and aesthetic climax of the concert. Composed by the legendary violin maestro Lalgudi G. Jayaraman in the lilting, sweet Ragam Mand (Maand).

Driven by crisp rhythmic sollukattus (percussive syllables like "Ta Dhim Ta Na"), brisk swara passages, and playful cross-rhythmic syncopations, this piece delivers an exhilarating, synchronized finale filled with celebratory ecstasy.

Ragam: Mand (Maand)
Talam: Adi Talam
Composer: Lalgudi G. Jayaraman

11. Harivarasanam & Pavamana Mangalam

Benediction 11

The sacred conclusion of the concert. It begins with "Harivarasanam", the celestial lullaby sung every night at the Sabarimala sanctum, and concludes with the auspicious Tyagaraja Mangalam "Pavamana".

Rendered in the supreme peace of Ragam Madhyamavathi—which according to tradition dissolves any tonal imbalances and leaves only harmony—the recital concludes with prayers of gratitude to the Gurus, ancestors, and rasikas for universal peace and prosperity.

Ragam: Madhyamavathi & Saurashtram
Talam: Adi Talam
Composer: Kumbakudi Kulathur Iyer & Saint Tyagaraja

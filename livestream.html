Viewing Guide

Viewing Tips for Rasikas

  • Audio Setup: For the best classical concert experience, listen via external speakers or high-fidelity headphones to appreciate the subtle microtonal gamakas and rich mridangam naadham.
  • Live Chat & Wishes: You can send your blessings, live comments, and congratulations to Sahana, Guru Smt. Geetha Ravi, and the ensemble through the YouTube live chat.
  • Follow Along with Program Guide: Browse the song-by-song lyrics, ragam scales, and sahityam meanings on our Program Guide page during the recital.
  • Recording Availability: The recording will remain archived on this page and on YouTube for on-demand playback following the concert.
